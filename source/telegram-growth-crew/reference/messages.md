# Writing messages to channel admins

One message per admin, written for that channel. 60–120 words.

## Must have
- Their language (Spanish channel → Spanish).
- One specific detail about their channel (topic, a recent post theme, its title). No copy-paste template feel.
- One line about the owner's channel: name and what it is about. Use only numbers the owner gave you.
- What you ask:
  - **Price request:** price for 1 post that stays 24 hours at the top, and whether there is a 3-post package. Ask for their recent views per post.
  - **Swap:** a free cross-promo: each channel posts a short shout-out for the other at an agreed time, both clearly marked. Offer to share stats first. Best for channels of similar size.
  - **Counter-offer:** when they named a price, compare it to their views. Price per 1,000 views is (price ÷ views) × 1,000; crypto channels often land around $8–20 per 1,000 views; the `fair_price_usd` column in the Leads sheet has the range for each channel. Propose a fair number or a package.
- Say that the paid post will be clearly marked as an ad.
- End with one clear question.

## Never
- Pressure, flattery spam, or fake urgency.
- Promises of profit, signals or financial advice.
- Pretending to be someone else, or claiming numbers you were not given.
- More than one emoji.

## Format to give the owner

**@channel_username** → admin: @admin_contact · *Price request*

```
<the message>
```
