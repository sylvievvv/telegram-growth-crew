Telegram Growth Crew

Your own Claude, working as a small crew that finds channels to advertise in, checks them for fakes, writes to their admins, and shows which ads really brought subscribers.

1Meet the crew

Scout

Finds crypto Telegram channels that fit you and collects their real numbers: subscribers, views per post, growth, and the admin contact.

Checker

Gives every channel a fraud score, so you don't pay for fake subscribers or bought views.

Negotiator

Writes one personal message per admin: asking prices, making counter-offers or proposing free swaps. You send it.

Tracker

Works out what each new subscriber cost, per ad, and tells you which channels to book again.

Strategist

Looks at your own channel stats and suggests 3 concrete experiments to grow faster.

Broadcaster

Writes crypto posts for your own channel: a weekly scam alert, 60-second explainers and a facts-only weekly recap. It looks up this week's news first. Nothing goes out until you approve.

2Install (one time)

  1. Open claude.ai or the Claude app on a computer. Works on the free plan; Pro or Max gives more room for big searches.
  2. Go to Settings → Capabilities and turn on Code execution and file creation.
  3. Find Skills (under Customize, or on the same Settings page), click Upload skill and choose telegram-growth-crew.zip. Don't unzip it first.
  4. Check that the switch next to telegram-growth-crew is on.
  5. In a chat, make sure web search is on. It's in the tools menu next to the message box.

3Your first 10 minutes

Open a new chat and type:

start

Claude asks three questions. Answer them in one message, for example:

@mychannel, crypto education, English, 18k subs. Ads in Spanish and English channels, DeFi and news. $30–120 per post, $1000 a month.

Claude saves this, immediately finds your first channels, and gives you two files. The first is agent-world.html: open it to see your crew as 3D ninjas walking to their stations and playing back what they just did, with tabs for your leads, ad results, approval inbox and daily report. The second is growth-crew.xlsx, your lists. Its sheets:

  • Start here: how to use the file
  • My setup: your answers
  • Leads: every channel found, green / yellow / red
  • Ad log: every ad, with cost per new subscriber
  • Drafts, Activity, Ideas: messages waiting for you, what the crew did, and growth ideas

Keep this file. Next time, attach growth-crew.xlsx to the chat and just say what you want. Claude remembers everything through it. Easiest option: make a Claude Project called "Telegram Growth" and put the file in the project, so it's always there.

Crypto posts for your own channel:

Write this week's scam alert Explain staking in 60 seconds Weekly crypto recap Plan my posts for next week

The crew knows crypto (DeFi, wallets, Layer 2, stablecoins, scams) and searches the web for this week's news. It never gives buy/sell tips or price predictions.

5How the fraud check works

The most important number is reach: how many of a channel's subscribers actually see a post. Healthy crypto channels reach 8–30%. A channel with 100,000 subscribers and 100 views per post is fake or dead. Paying it is money thrown away.

ScoreVerdictWhat to do
0–29cleanGood to contact
30–59check by handScroll the channel's last 20 posts before paying
60–100riskyDon't buy

The score also looks at sudden subscriber jumps, big drops, views with no reactions, and signal/VIP channels. Claude also opens each channel's public Telegram page to check that the recent views are real.

6What's a fair price?

Every channel gets a fair price range for one post, based on its real views. Crypto ads usually cost $8–20 per 1,000 views. A channel with 3,000 views per post is worth about $24–60 per post. If an admin asks much more, Claude writes a polite counter-offer.

Judge ads by cost per new subscriber, not by price per post. A $20 ad that brings 95 people ($0.21 each) beats a $300 ad that brings 410 ($0.73 each).

7Give every ad its own invite link

  1. In Telegram, open your channel → Manage / Edit → Invite links → Create a new link.
  2. Name it after the channel that will post your ad, for example ad_criptonoticias_oct.
  3. Send that link to the admin. Only this link goes in their post.
  4. A week later, open Invite links again. Each link shows how many people joined. Tell Claude the cost and the number of joins.

9Safety rules built in

  • Claude never sends messages and never pays anyone. It prepares; you decide.
  • No mass messaging: at most 10 personal messages per run.
  • No fake accounts, bought views or tricks that can get your channel banned.
  • Paid posts are always marked as ads.
  • No buy/sell signals or financial advice.

10Questions

Claude says it can't find the skill or the file.

Check that the skill switch is on in Settings → Capabilities, and that "Code execution and file creation" is on. Start a new chat and type start.

Claude asks my setup questions again.

It didn't see your growth-crew.xlsx. Attach it to the chat, or put it in your Claude Project.

Some channels have no admin contact.

Many channels only list it in a pinned post. Open the channel in Telegram and look at the pinned message or the description.

Can it post to my channel or message admins automatically?

Messages to admins: never automatically, because that gets Telegram accounts banned; you send them yourself. Posting to your own channel: yes, with option B or C (section 8), and always only after you approve.

Can new members get "Welcome %username%!"?

Yes. Ask Claude: Write a welcome message: Welcome %username%!. %username% becomes their @name. With option B or C, everyone who joins through your ad links gets it in a private chat, and new members of your discussion group are greeted there. One Telegram rule: no bot can message people who join a public channel straight through its @name. They see your pinned welcome post instead.

Will my existing copy/paste bot keep working?

Yes. Option A doesn't touch it. Option B only uses it to send the posts you approve. Option C uses a separate, new bot.

Are the numbers always right?

They come from public Telemetr pages and Telegram's own channel previews. They're a strong filter, not a guarantee. Before a big payment, scroll the channel yourself for a minute.
