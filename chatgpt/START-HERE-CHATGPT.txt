TELEGRAM GROWTH CREW FOR CHATGPT - READ ME FIRST
================================================

What this is
------------
Your own GPT in ChatGPT that works as a small crew to grow your crypto
Telegram channel:

  Scout        finds crypto channels that sell ads, with their real numbers
  Checker      spots fake subscribers and bought views before you pay
  Negotiator   writes personal messages to channel admins (you send them)
  Tracker      shows which ads brought subscribers, and what each one cost
  Strategist   growth ideas from your own channel stats
  Broadcaster  crypto posts (scam alerts, explainers, recaps) and your
               "Welcome %username%!" message

You need ChatGPT Plus (or higher) to make your own GPT.
No API keys, no server, no programmer needed.


Make the GPT (one time, about 5 minutes)
----------------------------------------
Do this on a computer, at chatgpt.com.

1. In the left menu, click "GPTs", then "+ Create" (top right).
2. Click the "Configure" tab. Do not use the "Create" chat tab.
3. Fill in:
   Name:         Telegram Growth Crew
   Description:  Grows my crypto Telegram channel: finds ad channels,
                 checks for fakes, drafts messages, tracks ads.
   Instructions: open 1-INSTRUCTIONS.txt, copy ALL the text, and paste it.
4. Conversation starters (add these 4):
   start
   Find 15 Spanish DeFi channels to advertise in
   Write this week's scam alert
   Write a welcome message: Welcome %username%!
5. Knowledge: click "Upload files" and choose ALL 7 files in the folder
   2-KNOWLEDGE-FILES:
     crew.py
     agent-world-template.html
     crew-guide.md
     fraud-rules.md
     messages.md
     ad-tracking.md
     crypto-content.md
6. Capabilities: turn ON
     - Web Search
     - Code Interpreter & Data Analysis   (this one is required!)
   Image generation can be off.
7. Click "Create" (top right). Choose "Only me", then "Save".


First use
---------
Open your new GPT and type:   start

It asks three short questions: your channel, where you want to advertise,
and your budget. Answer in one message. It then finds your first channels
and gives you two files to download:

  growth-crew.xlsx   your lists: setup, channels, ads, drafts, activity
  agent-world.html   your crew as 3D ninjas, replaying what they just did,
                     with tabs for leads, ads, inbox and report.
                     Download it and open it in your browser.

KEEP growth-crew.xlsx. Next time, attach it to the chat (paperclip) and just
say what you want. That file is the crew's memory.


If something goes wrong
-----------------------
- "crew.py missing": attach crew.py and agent-world-template.html (from the
  folder 2-KNOWLEDGE-FILES) to the chat once, and say "try again".
- It doesn't find channels: make sure Web Search is on in the GPT settings.
- No download link: say "give me the download links for both files".


Safety
------
The crew never sends messages or pays anyone. It only prepares; you decide
and send. No fake accounts, no bought views, no buy/sell signals, and paid
posts are always marked as ads.
